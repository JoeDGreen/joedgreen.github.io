Privacy Policy for Joe's Harness

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Joe's Harness is a personal software tool built and used by one person, Joe Green, on his own computers. It is not a public service and it has no other users.

What the app does

At its owner's direction, the app can connect to the owner's own Google Drive account to list, read, download, and upload files, using Google's sign-in consent (OAuth). The owner signs in with Google directly; the app never sees or stores the owner's Google password.

What data is stored, and where

Sign-in tokens issued by Google, and any file content the owner asks the app to read or download, are stored only on the owner's own computers. Nothing is uploaded to, processed by, or shared with the developer or any third party. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data sharing

None. The only network communication involved is between the owner's computer and Google's own Drive service, carrying out the owner's own requests on the owner's own files.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time from inside the app, which deletes the stored tokens from his computers, and can also revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
