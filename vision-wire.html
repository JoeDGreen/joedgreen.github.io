Vision Wire

Vision Wire is the blindness community's news wire — the week's stories in blindness and disability, assistive technology, and AI as it touches blind life, plus the policy fights, new gear, and community news that matter. Hosted by Joe Green, technology enthusiast, with guests, clips, and straight talk. A JDG Productions podcast.
