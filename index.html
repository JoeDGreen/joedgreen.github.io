About Joe

Joe is a director on the board of the California Council of the Blind, a hobbyist DJ, an amateur radio student, and a lifelong technology enthusiast. Everything under JDG Productions comes from first-hand experience: the tools he builds with, the gear on his desk, the apps that pass — and fail — his screen reader tests. If it is on this site, he has tried it himself.

Access Granted — the podcast

Now live — trailer out now

Access Granted with Joe Green — a podcast about AI, assistive technology, accessible arts, and the honest results of building things as a blind creator. Every episode is a first-hand verdict, in four parts:

  • Building with AI — making software, voices, and tools with AI, and reporting what really happened.
  • The Test Bench — hands-on tests of apps, recorders, microphones, and gear, including product samples.
  • Accessible Arts & Community — accessible theatre, community events, and the people making them happen.
  • The Scrapyard — the projects that didn't work out, and what they taught him. No hype, no pretending.

Listen now: Hear the trailer, "Welcome to Access Granted" (MP3, 34 seconds). Find Access Granted on Spotify and on Apple Podcasts. Podcast apps can also subscribe through the show's RSS feed.

Produced by JDG Productions. Episode one is in the works.
