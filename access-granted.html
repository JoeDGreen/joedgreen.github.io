S1E2 - Aura in the App Store

Published Thursday, November 5, 2026 · Running time 17 minutes 45 seconds

On October 27, I decided to build the meeting app I wished existed — one a blind host can run from start to finish. Nine days later, Apple said yes.

In this episode: why I named it Aura (it's not something you see — it's a presence you feel), what Aura Meetings does — your own personal meeting number, hosting from your phone, joining by phone, meeting notes in your inbox — the six-day wait while Apple reviewed it, and the moment the "Welcome to the App Store" email arrived. Plus what's next: version 1.1 in testing, and breakout rooms on the way.

Aura Meetings is free for iPhone and iPad: https://apps.apple.com/app/aura-meetings/id6829037636 You can also join any Aura meeting by phone: +1 (916) 602-0121.

S1E1 - I Cloned My Voice, and It Didn't Work Out

Published Monday, October 26, 2026 · Running time 10 minutes 46 seconds

What happens when a totally blind builder clones his own voice, locally, on his own Mac - no cloud, nothing leaving the machine? Joe Green wanted a tool that would read long documents aloud in his own voice, so he built one: Whisper to turn speech into text, a local AI model (Qwen 3.5) to clean the text up, and Chatterbox to speak it back in his cloned voice - all put together with Claude Code.

Then came the test. Takes that stopped mid-sentence. A clone that came back sounding "20 times lighter - like Alvin and the Chipmunks." In this episode: what he built, what went wrong, the lesson he'd carry into a second attempt (record line by line, get a better model) - and why he's glad he tried it anyway, plus his message to blind creators who are afraid to start.
