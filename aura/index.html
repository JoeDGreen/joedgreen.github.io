Your Aura account

Anyone can join a room as a guest. To create and host a room, you need a free account — that is how Aura knows the room is yours and gives you the host controls. Your account keeps you signed in on this device: using Aura renews your sign-in, so you only type your password again after 90 days away.

      Keyboard shortcuts

      • Mute or unmute yourself: Alt+A on Windows; Option+A or Command+A on Mac
      • Raise or lower your hand: Alt+Y on Windows; Option+Y or Command+Y on Mac
      • Leave the room: Alt+Q on Windows; Option+Q on Mac (Command+Q belongs to the browser itself — it quits the browser, so it cannot be used here)
      • Mute everyone's microphones (host and co-hosts): Alt+M on Windows; Option+M on Mac
      • Start or stop recording (host and co-hosts): Alt+R on Windows; Option+R on Mac
